## Recent projects

**Agentic AI platform.** Multi-provider AI agents that work on a shared visual canvas. I work on the tools registry,
AI engine, conversation hub and the runtime that lets agents work next to people.

**AI Voice Mode.** A real-time, speech-to-speech AI that people talk to while they work on the canvas: it listens,
answers and acts on the board. As an engineer on the team I worked on sub-second voice-to-voice latency,
interruption handling, audio pipelines over WebRTC, and turning spoken intent into tool calls. The design splits a
fast voice agent that only converses from the agent platform that does the work behind it. Built with OpenAI on
their Realtime API, it went from kickoff to the Canvas '26 keynotes in San Francisco, London, Sydney and Tokyo in
about ten weeks.

**Monetization platform integration.** Integrated Stigg as Miro's system of record for plans, entitlements and
usage, replacing the in-house monetization system. This included a credit-based AI pricing model: a monthly AI
allowance for free users, more per seat for paid users, with usage metered, enforced and reset automatically
across front-end and back-end. Tens of millions of subscriptions were migrated without disruption, and AI pricing
launched in under 6 weeks. [Read the case study](https://www.stigg.io/customers/miro).
